# NumidAI — Hero Cinematic Video Concept (Seedance 2.0)

_A homepage hero animation telling one story: an architectural project evolving through AI.
**Do not generate yet.** Reference the approved hero renders (A1/A2) so the building stays
identical across the sequence._

**Story arc:**
> Blueprint → Climate Analysis → AI Optimization → Sustainable Building → Performance Dashboard

**Format:** 16:9, ~14–18s total, 1080p, no audio, slow and precise, suitable for either autoplay
loop or scroll-scrubbing. Engineering-trust tone — calm, not flashy. No text baked in, no logos,
no people, no fast cuts, no camera shake.

**Master visual language:** one continuous MENA building, same structure throughout; deep teal /
emerald / turquoise / sage / warm ivory; thin blueprint construction lines as the connective
motif between phases; light warms gradually from technical-cool to golden as the building
"resolves" and thrives.

---

## Scene 1 — Blueprint (0.0–3.0s)
- **Visual:** the building exists only as a precise blueprint/wireframe — thin turquoise
  construction lines, dimension marks, axis guides, faint grid on warm ivory.
- **Camera:** slow, steady push-in along the axis; a gentle parallax as lines resolve.
- **Motion language:** lines "draw" and settle; a single soft AI scan line passes vertically once.
- **Lighting:** flat, cool, technical.
- **Transition out:** the wireframe lines begin to gain faint surface — dissolve toward Scene 2.

## Scene 2 — Climate Analysis (3.0–6.5s)
- **Visual:** climate data overlays bloom over the still-semi-wireframe massing — a low→high heat
  map washes across the facades, solar-path arcs sweep, wind-flow streamlines curl around the
  form, small floating data callouts (temperature, comfort) fade in.
- **Camera:** slow orbit ~15–20° around the building to reveal it in three dimensions.
- **Motion language:** analytical, layered, precise — overlays settle, never flicker.
- **Lighting:** cool with the first hint of warmth entering frame.
- **Transition out:** heat-map cools as "optimizations" begin — cross-dissolve.

## Scene 3 — AI Optimization (6.5–10.0s)
- **Visual:** the building visibly adapts — shading louvers rotate into position, vertical
  planting bands grow across the facade in accelerated time-lapse, glazing/orientation subtly
  refine. A restrained scan/grid pass reinforces "AI at work."
- **Camera:** continue the slow orbit, easing toward a hero 3/4 framing.
- **Motion language:** transformation is smooth and controlled — same building improving, not
  swapping.
- **Lighting:** warming noticeably; harsh flatness gives way to soft directional light.
- **Transition out:** materials fully resolve — dissolve to full photoreal.

## Scene 4 — Sustainable Building (10.0–13.5s)
- **Visual:** the fully rendered, climate-adapted building at golden hour — thriving greenery,
  active shading, native planting, calm and resolved. The "after" state.
- **Camera:** a slow drone-style pull-back and gentle rise over the building.
- **Motion language:** settled, confident, breathing — the payoff shot.
- **Lighting:** full warm golden hour, soft shadows, gentle atmosphere.
- **Transition out:** a floating analytics panel begins to assemble in the negative space.

## Scene 5 — Performance Dashboard (13.5–17.0s)
- **Visual:** thin glowing data lines and soft particles assemble the NumidAI dashboard UI over
  the building — a project card, sustainability score gauge settling to its value, four stat
  tiles, a small climate/radar chart. Restrained and precise, not sci-fi.
- **Camera:** settle to a stable, composed final frame with room for the hero headline overlay.
- **Motion language:** the graph settles into place rather than pulsing; everything comes to rest.
- **Lighting:** warm building behind, clean UI in front.
- **End state:** a calm, composed hero frame — building + resolved analytics, headline space at
  left (or right in RTL).

---

## Constraints (hard)
- One consistent building from blueprint to final; no scene swaps to different structures.
- No text, no logos, no people, no fast cuts, no shaky camera, no neon, no chaotic particles.
- Keep clean negative space for the headline and CTAs throughout the final frame.
- Slow, deliberate camera; realistic light grading; credibility over spectacle.
