# NumidAI — Brand Kit

_The single source of truth for NumidAI brand identity. Read this before writing copy, designing
any surface, or generating any asset._

---

## 1. What NumidAI is (and is not)

**NumidAI is** an AI-powered decision-support platform that helps architects, engineering firms,
real estate developers, construction companies, and government institutions across the Middle
East and North Africa (MENA) design sustainable, climate-responsive buildings. It analyzes
projects with AI and returns concrete, quantified recommendations that improve environmental
performance while reducing carbon emissions, operational cost, and energy consumption.

**NumidAI is not:**
- a construction company
- a material manufacturer or supplier
- a general contractor
- a BIM authoring tool (BIM is a future *integration*, not the core product)

The name evokes **Numidia** — the ancient North African civilization — signaling regional roots,
endurance, and mastery of building in a demanding climate, fused with **AI**.

---

## 2. Mission & Vision

**Mission:** Make sustainable architecture easier, faster, more accurate, and more accessible
across the MENA region.

**Vision:** A built environment in MENA where every new building is designed climate-first —
where carbon, energy, and comfort are optimized before the first stone is laid, and where AI
makes world-class sustainable design the default, not the exception.

---

## 3. Brand positioning

> **The AI decision-support layer for climate-responsive architecture in MENA.**

NumidAI sits between the design intent and the built result. It is the intelligence that tells a
professional — before construction — how a building will perform against the region's real
climate, and exactly what to change to make it better.

Positioning pillars:
1. **Region-specific.** Built for MENA climate, materials, typologies, and regulations — not a
   generic Western sustainability tool retrofitted for the desert.
2. **Decision-support, not decoration.** Every output is an actionable, quantified engineering
   recommendation, not a vague "AI insight."
3. **Enterprise-grade trust.** Credible enough for government institutions and large developers
   to stake capital decisions on.
4. **Sustainability as performance.** Carbon, energy, cost, and comfort framed as measurable
   performance metrics, not moral messaging.

## 4. Target audience

Primary users (all professionals evaluating a serious enterprise tool):
- Architects
- Urban planners
- Engineering firms
- Real estate developers
- Construction companies
- Government agencies / public institutions
- Universities (research & teaching)

Audience mindset: technically literate, outcome-driven, accountable for capital-intensive
decisions. They distrust hype and reward precision. Copy and UI must never talk down to them.

---

## 5. Value proposition

**Core:** Design climate-adapted buildings that cut carbon, energy, and cost — with AI analysis
that would otherwise take a specialist team weeks, delivered in a fraction of the time.

Value by outcome:
- **Lower carbon** — embodied + operational carbon estimated and reduced across the life cycle
- **Lower energy** — cooling load and energy-use intensity optimized for the local climate
- **Lower cost** — operational and life-cycle cost reduced through passive-first design
- **Higher comfort** — thermal comfort hours increased through simulation-driven design
- **Faster decisions** — analysis, alternatives, and reports generated in hours, not weeks

Value by audience:
- **Architects / firms:** validate and optimize design intent against real climate data early.
- **Developers:** de-risk projects, evidence sustainability claims, protect asset value.
- **Construction companies:** anticipate performance and material implications before build.
- **Government:** enforce and encourage climate-adapted design at policy scale.
- **Universities:** teach and research climate-responsive design with a real analysis engine.

## 6. Tone of voice

Confident, precise, calm, credible. Closer to an aerospace or advanced-engineering software
company than to a consumer app or an advocacy nonprofit.

**Do:**
- Lead with mechanism *and* outcome together ("AI climate simulation identifies where shading
  cuts cooling load").
- Use real units and metric names (kWh/m²/year, kg CO₂/m², % thermal comfort hours).
- Write short, declarative, technically accurate sentences.
- Treat the reader as an expert.

**Don't:**
- Use consumer-app cheerfulness ("Let's get you started! 🎉").
- Make vague claims ("AI helps you build better").
- Present illustrative figures as universal guarantees.
- Use climate guilt or alarmist framing — NumidAI sells performance, not fear.

Voice in one line: **"Precise. Regional. Proven by the numbers."**

## 8. Visual identity (summary — full detail in `docs/design-system.md`)

- **Aesthetic:** premium enterprise engineering software. Blueprint-inspired, architecturally
  precise, calm. The bar is Autodesk / Arcadis / NVIDIA / Apple / Stripe / Linear / Notion /
  BIG / Foster + Partners — never a generic AI-startup landing page.
- **Signature motif:** blueprint / construction-line graphics (dimension marks, axis guides,
  wireframe-to-render reveals). This is the visual DNA that separates NumidAI from generic AI
  products, and echoes the construction-line treatment already present in the wordmark.
- **Imagery:** custom MENA-informed architectural renders only. Never stock photography.

### Color palette (authoritative)

| Token | Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--primary` | Deep Forest Teal | `#0D3B3E` | Headings, nav, primary buttons, dashboard chrome |
| `--secondary` | Emerald Teal | `#156B6A` | Secondary elements, hover states |
| `--accent` | Turquoise | `#2AA69A` | Active states, links, data-highlight lines, small icons |
| `--soft-green` | Sage Green | `#A8C5A1` | Vegetation/nature motifs, supporting accent |
| `--bg` | Warm Ivory | `#F5F2E8` | Primary background |
| `--text` | Charcoal Green | `#24352F` | Body text |
| `--neutral` | Mist Gray | `#D9DFDB` | Borders, dividers, disabled states |
| `--highlight` | Soft Gold | `#D6B55D` | Rare premium accent — 1–2 per section max, never a base |

Hard rules: no colors outside this set; no neon; no purple/violet "AI gradient." Soft Gold is
strictly an accent.

### Typography

- **Headings:** large geometric sans (Space Grotesk / General Sans / Inter Display class) —
  minimal, editorial, luxury-enterprise weight.
- **Body:** clean humanist sans (Inter).
- **Data / labels / metrics:** a mono or tabular-figure treatment for numbers and units.
- **Arabic:** a genuine geometric Arabic typeface (IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Noto Kufi Arabic or
  equivalent). Never fake RTL by flipping a Latin font.

### Iconography

- Thin, precise, line-based icons with consistent stroke weight — engineering/architectural
  drawing feel, not filled consumer glyphs or generic "robot/circuit" AI clichés.
- Nature and climate icons (leaf, sun, wind, water, thermal) rendered in the same line system.
- The emblem (twin-leaf sprout inside stacked diamonds) is the nature-meets-structure motif —
  use as favicon, app icon, and standalone mark.

### Logo usage

- Use the supplied **wordmark** (`assets/logos/numidai-wordmark.png`) in headers and marketing.
  Do not regenerate or redraw it.
- Use the supplied **emblem** (`assets/logos/numidai-emblem.jpeg`) for favicon, app icon, and
  compact/standalone contexts.
- Preserve generous clear space around both; never place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Never stretch, recolor outside the palette, add effects, or rotate the marks.
- A full logo-usage spec (clear space, min sizes, mono versions, misuse examples) is a required
  future asset — see `asset-plan.md`.

## 9. UI design principles (summary)

12-column grid, generous spacing, restrained rounded corners, glassmorphism only where earned
(floating analytics over a render), blueprint backgrounds and architectural line graphics as a
recurring motif, subtle micro-interactions. Every screen must pass the test: _could this be a
real Autodesk / Arcadis / Foster + Partners tool or case study?_ Full detail in
`docs/design-system.md`.

---

## 10. Motion design principles (summary)

Precise and engineered, never flashy. Flagship pattern: blueprint-to-building reveal. Supporting:
AI scan lines, floating analytics settling into place, climate heatmap fades, single-run count-up
counters, calm dashboard transitions, subtle hover lifts. Minimal overall; respect
`prefers-reduced-motion`. Full detail in `docs/design-system.md`.

---

## 11. Languages

- **Arabic** is a first-class primary language with full RTL layout and mirrored motion.
- **English** is available via header toggle (LTR).
- Arabic copy is written idiomatically for meaning — never a literal word-for-word translation.
- EN and AR builds ship in parity; an English-only pass is not "done."
